Methodology

Wealth velocity vs. cash income

W-2 income is a flow of cash paid for labor hours. Wealth velocity is the change in market value of equity, options, dividends and real assets. They're different things — TICKS compares them to show scale, not to claim billionaires are paid this as salary.

Velocity formula

Velocity = ΔNet Worth / 31,536,000 s

31,536,000 = 365 × 24 × 3,600 seconds in a standard year.

Calculation modes

  • 5-Year Annualized (default): (NWt − NWt−5) / 5. Smooths volatility.
  • TTM: NWt − NWt−1. Captures recent momentum.

Class parity time

T = Annual Income / Velocity

Seconds for a billionaire to accumulate a full year of a tier's income.
